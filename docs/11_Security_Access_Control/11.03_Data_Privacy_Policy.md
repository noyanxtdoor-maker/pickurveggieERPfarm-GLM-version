# 11.03 Data Privacy Policy

## Purpose

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 shall protect all company, employee, customer, supplier, and operational data through strict access control, encryption, auditing, and privacy enforcement.

Data access shall follow the principles of:

- Least privilege
- Need-to-know access
- Data minimization
- Accountability
- Confidentiality

---

# Data Ownership

All information stored within PickUrVeggie ERP V3 belongs to the company operating the system.

Users are granted access only according to their:

- Company membership
- Branch assignments
- Role
- Permissions

Access rights do not imply ownership of data.

---

# Branch Data Privacy

Branch users shall only access records belonging to their authorized branches.

Example:

Siargao Branch Worker:

Allowed:

- View assigned tasks
- View personal attendance
- View personal payroll records if permitted

Not allowed:

- View Cebu employee records
- View Cebu payroll
- View other branches' inventory
- View company-wide financial reports

---

# Employee Privacy

Employee information shall be classified by sensitivity.

## Basic Employment Information

Examples:

- Name
- Position
- Work schedules
- Assigned branch

Accessible by:

- Authorized supervisors
- Human Resources
- Management

---

## Confidential Employment Information

Examples:

- Salary
- Payroll history
- Government identification numbers
- Tax information
- Contracts
- Disciplinary records

Accessible only by:

- Authorized HR personnel
- Corporate Accountant (financial information only)
- Owner and authorized executives

---

# Financial Data Privacy

Financial records shall be protected according to responsibility.

Examples:

Branch Supervisor:

May access:

- Approved branch operating budgets
- Branch expenses related to their authority

May not access:

- Company-wide profits
- Other branch financial records
- Executive financial information

---

# Customer and Supplier Data Protection

Customer and supplier records shall be accessible only to users with business requirements.

Examples:

Sales personnel:

- Customer orders
- Delivery information
- Sales history

Purchasing personnel:

- Supplier information
- Purchase history
- Contract records

Unnecessary access shall be denied.

---

# AI Data Access Policy

AI assistants shall operate under the same security rules as human users.

AI shall only access:

- Data available to the currently authenticated user
- Information required to complete the requested task

AI shall never:

- Bypass branch restrictions
- Reveal confidential payroll information
- Expose hidden financial records
- Override user permissions

---

# File and Document Privacy

Documents may include:

- Employee contracts
- Invoices
- Receipts
- Certificates
- Permits
- Legal documents

Each document shall maintain:

- Company ownership
- Branch ownership
- Access permissions
- Upload history
- Modification history

---

# Data Encryption and Protection

The system shall implement appropriate security measures including:

- Encrypted communication between devices and servers
- Secure authentication mechanisms
- Protected storage of sensitive information
- Secure backup procedures

Passwords shall never be stored as plain text.

Authentication credentials shall be securely hashed using modern industry standards.

---

# Audit and Monitoring

The system shall maintain audit records for sensitive actions.

Examples:

- Viewing confidential records
- Modifying payroll
- Changing user permissions
- Exporting sensitive reports
- Downloading confidential documents

Audit records shall capture:

- User identity
- Company
- Branch
- Action performed
- Date and time
- Device or session information when available

---

# Data Retention and Recovery

Business records shall be retained according to company policy and applicable legal requirements.

The system shall support:

- Backup and recovery
- Historical records
- Restoration of authorized data
- Protection against accidental deletion

Deleted records should be archived when required instead of permanently removed.

---

# User Responsibilities

All users shall:

- Protect their login credentials
- Use the system only for authorized purposes
- Report suspicious activities
- Avoid sharing confidential company information

Unauthorized access, data extraction, or misuse of company data shall be recorded and subject to disciplinary action.

---

# Architecture Rule

No module, API, AI service, mobile application, or external integration shall access, expose, or transfer data without verifying:

- User identity
- Company authorization
- Branch authorization
- Role permissions
- Data sensitivity level

Data privacy enforcement is mandatory throughout the entire PickUrVeggie ERP V3 ecosystem.
