# 20.02 Multi-Tenant Company Structure

## Purpose

The multi-tenant foundation defines how companies and branches are organized inside PickUrVeggie ERP V3.

The system must support:

- Multiple independent companies
- Multiple branches per company
- Branch-level operations
- Corporate-level management
- Future expansion to hundreds of branches

---

# Company Entity

The `companies` table represents a legal business organization using the ERP.

Examples:

- SmartIsland AgriTech Farm
- PickUrVeggie Corporation
- ABC Agricultural Cooperative

---

## companies Table

Fields:

- id (UUID, Primary Key)
- company_code (Unique Business Code)
- company_name
- legal_name
- registration_number
- tax_identification_number
- company_type
  - Corporation
  - Partnership
  - Sole Proprietorship
  - Cooperative
  - Government Entity
  - Non-Profit Organization

- industry_type
- primary_contact_name
- primary_contact_email
- primary_contact_phone

- country
- province
- city
- address

- status
  - Active
  - Inactive
  - Suspended
  - Archived

Audit Fields:

- created_at
- updated_at
- created_by
- updated_by

---

# Branch Entity

A branch represents a physical operational location under a company.

Examples:

Company:
SmartIsland AgriTech Farm

Branches:

- Siargao Farm
- Cebu Farm
- Bohol Farm

Each branch maintains independent:

- Inventory
- Production
- Employees
- Payroll
- Sales
- Expenses
- Equipment
- Documents

---

## branches Table

Fields:

- id (UUID, Primary Key)
- company_id (Foreign Key → companies.id)

Identification:

- branch_code
- branch_name
- branch_type

Examples of branch types:

- Farm
- Warehouse
- Processing Facility
- Retail Store
- Office
- Distribution Center

Location:

- country
- province
- city
- address
- latitude
- longitude

Operational Settings:

- timezone
- currency
- language

Management:

- branch_manager_user_id
- opening_date
- closing_date

Status:

- Active
- Inactive
- Under Construction
- Closed

Audit Fields:

- created_at
- updated_at
- created_by
- updated_by

---

# Branch Independence Principle

A branch is treated as an independent operational unit.

Branch data must never be mixed accidentally.

Examples:

Siargao Branch:

Inventory:
- Calcium Nitrate: 100 kg
- Lettuce Seeds: 50 packets


Cebu Branch:

Inventory:
- Calcium Nitrate: 25 kg
- Lettuce Seeds: 10 packets


The system shall maintain separate:

- Inventory balances
- Employee records
- Payroll records
- Production records
- Financial performance

---

# Company-Level Consolidation

Authorized corporate users may view consolidated information.

Examples:

Company Dashboard:

All Branch Revenue:

Siargao:
₱1,500,000

Cebu:
₱900,000


Total Company Revenue:

₱2,400,000

The system shall support:

- Individual branch reports
- Multi-branch comparisons
- Company-wide analytics
- Consolidated financial statements

---

# Branch Hierarchy and Expansion

The architecture shall support future structures such as:

Company

├── Region: Visayas

│   ├── Siargao Farm

│   ├── Cebu Farm

│

├── Region: Luzon

│   ├── Batangas Farm

│   ├── Laguna Warehouse


Future database extensions may include:

- Regional management
- Franchise operations
- International branches
- Multi-currency support

---

# Branch Creation and Administration

Only authorized corporate roles may create or manage branches.

Examples:

Allowed:

- Developer
- Owner
- Co-Owner
- General Manager


Not Allowed:

- Branch Supervisor
- Worker
- Standard Operator

All branch creation and changes shall be recorded in the audit system.

---

# Company and Branch Settings

The architecture shall support configurable settings.

Examples:

Company Settings:

- Accounting preferences
- Fiscal year
- Tax settings
- Global policies
- AI configuration


Branch Settings:

- Operating hours
- Local rules
- Production schedules
- Inventory thresholds
- Notification preferences

---

# Security Relationship

Every operational record must follow:

Company
    ↓
Branch
    ↓
User Branch Role
    ↓
Permission
    ↓
Allowed Action

The database shall enforce these relationships through:

- Foreign keys
- Row Level Security (RLS)
- Audit logging

---

# Architecture Rule

No operational data may exist without being linked to its owning company and appropriate branch.

Any table that represents branch operations must contain:

- company_id
- branch_id

This rule is mandatory throughout the entire PickUrVeggie ERP V3 database.
