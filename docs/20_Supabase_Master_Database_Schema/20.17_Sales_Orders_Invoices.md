# 20.17 Sales Orders and Invoices

## Purpose

The sales system manages customer orders, pricing, invoicing, payments, and revenue recognition.

All sales must maintain traceability to the products sold.

---

# Sales Workflow

Customer
    ↓
Sales Order
    ↓
Order Approval (if required)
    ↓
Inventory Reservation
    ↓
Invoice
    ↓
Payment
    ↓
Delivery
    ↓
Revenue Recording

---

# Sales Line Items

Each order line records:

- Finished goods batch
- Product
- Quantity
- Unit price
- Discount
- Total price

---

# Invoices

Invoices record the legal financial transaction.

The system supports:

- Cash sales
- Credit sales
- Partial payments
- Installment payments

---

# Accounts Receivable Integration

Credit sales automatically create customer receivables.

Example:

Restaurant purchases vegetables:

Invoice:
₱50,000

Payment:
30 days later

The ERP records:

- Customer debt
- Payment due date
- Payment history

---

# Revenue Integrity Rule

A sale cannot exceed available inventory.

Inventory must be reserved or deducted through controlled stock movements.

---
