# 20.11 Suppliers and Customers Ledger

## Purpose

The system maintains a centralized record of all business partners.

This includes:

- Suppliers
- Customers
- Contractors
- Service providers

A single entity may have multiple relationships.

Example:

ABC Agricultural Supply may be:

- Supplier of fertilizers
- Equipment maintenance contractor

---

# Business Partners Table

Table: business_partners

Fields:

Identification:

- id (UUID)
- company_id
- partner_code
- partner_type

Partner Types:

- Supplier
- Customer
- Contractor
- Service Provider
- Government Agency

---

Basic Information:

- business_name
- contact_person
- email
- mobile_number
- address
- country
- province
- city

---

Business Information:

- tax_identification_number
- payment_terms
- credit_limit

---

Relationship Status:

- Active

---

Audit Fields:

- created_by
- created_at

---

# Branch Relationship

Business partners belong to the company.

Example:

Supplier:
Hydro Farm Supply Center

Can supply:

- Siargao Branch
- Cebu Branch
- Bohol Branch

The supplier master record is shared.

Transactions remain branch-specific.

---

# Supplier Performance History

The ERP shall track:

- Total purchases
- Delivery performance
- Quality issues
- Price history
- Contract history

---

# Customer Relationship History

The ERP shall track:

- Sales history
- Purchase frequency
- Payment behavior
- Outstanding balances
- Preferred products

---

# Architecture Rule

The partner master record belongs to the company.

Purchases, sales, and financial transactions shall contain:

- company_id
- branch_id

to maintain proper branch ownership.
