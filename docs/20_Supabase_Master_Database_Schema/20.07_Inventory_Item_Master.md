# 20.07 Inventory Item Master

## Purpose

The Inventory Item Master is the central catalog of all materials, products, supplies, and assets used by the company.

The item master defines what an item is. It does not store current stock quantities.

Actual stock balances are determined through inventory batches and movement history.

# Company-Wide Item Master

The item definition belongs to the company.

Example:

Company:
SmartIsland AgriTech Farm


Item:
Calcium Nitrate

This item may exist in multiple branches:

Siargao:
100 kg

Cebu:
50 kg


The item record is shared, but inventory balances remain separated by branch.

# Inventory Security

Users may only access inventory records for authorized branches.

Example:

Siargao Operator:

Allowed:
- View Siargao inventory
- Record Siargao usage

Not Allowed:
- View Cebu inventory

---

# Future Support

The inventory architecture shall support:

- Barcode scanning
- QR code tracking
- RFID tracking
- Automatic IoT inventory monitoring
- AI-based stock forecasting

---

# Architecture Rule

The Inventory Item Master defines the identity of an item.

Actual quantity, location, ownership, and history shall always be tracked through inventory batches and stock movement records.
