# 20.16 Finished Goods Inventory

## Purpose

Finished Goods Inventory tracks harvested products that are ready for sale.

Unlike raw agricultural inputs, finished goods originate from harvest batches and maintain complete production traceability.

---

# Finished Goods Flow

Crop Block
    ↓
Harvest Batch
    ↓
Grading
    ↓
Packaging
    ↓
Finished Goods Inventory
    ↓

# Inventory Principle

Finished goods are inventory assets.

Their quantities are controlled through inventory movement records.

Manual quantity changes are prohibited.

---

# Architecture Rule

Every finished goods record must be linked to an original harvest batch.
