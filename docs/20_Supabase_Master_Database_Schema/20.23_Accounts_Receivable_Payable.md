# 20.23 Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable

## Purpose

The AR/AP system manages money owed to and by the company.

---

# Accounts Receivable (AR)

Represents customer debts.

Example:

Restaurant buys vegetables worth ₱30,000.

Payment Terms:
30 days

The system records:

- Customer
- Invoice
- Due date
- Outstanding balance
- Payment history

---

# accounts_receivable Table

Fields:

- id
- company_id
- branch_id
- customer_id
- invoice_id
- original_amount
- remaining_balance
- due_date
- status

Status:

- Open
- Partially Paid
- Paid
- Overdue
- Written Off

---

# Accounts Payable (AP)

Represents company debts to suppliers.

Example:

Fertilizer purchased on credit.

The system tracks:

- Supplier
- Purchase document
- Amount owed
- Due date

---

# accounts_payable Table

Fields:

- id
- company_id
- branch_id
- supplier_id
- purchase_reference_id
- original_amount
- remaining_balance
- due_date
- status

Status:

- Open
- Partially Paid
- Paid
- Overdue
- Disputed

---

# Payment Matching

Payments shall be applied against specific invoices or obligations.

The system shall maintain:

- Original balance
- Payment records
- Remaining balance

---

# Architecture Rule

A receivable or payable is not removed after payment.

Its historical record remains for audit and financial analysis.
