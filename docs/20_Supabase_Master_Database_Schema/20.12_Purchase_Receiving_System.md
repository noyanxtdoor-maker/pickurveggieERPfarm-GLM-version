# 20.12 Purchase Receiving System

## Purpose

The Purchase Receiving System controls how purchased materials enter inventory.

Inventory shall never increase simply because a purchase was created.

Stock increases only after a verified receiving transaction.

---

# Purchasing Workflow

Supplier
   ↓
Purchase Request
   ↓
Approval Workflow
   ↓
Purchase Order
   ↓
Delivery
   ↓
Receiving Inspection
   ↓
Inventory Batch Creation
   ↓
Stock Movement Entry

---

# Purchase Orders Table

Table: purchase_orders

Fields:

- id
- company_id
- branch_id
- supplier_id
- order_number
- order_date
- expected_delivery_date

Status:

- Draft
- Submitted
- Approved
- Ordered
- Partially Received
- Completed
- Cancelled

---

Financial Information:

- subtotal
- discount
- tax_amount
- shipping_cost
- total_amount

---

Audit Fields:

- created_by
- approved_by
- approved_at
- created_at
- updated_at

---

# Receiving Records Table

Table: purchase_receivings

Purpose:

Confirms what physically arrived.

Fields:

- id
- company_id
- branch_id
- purchase_order_id
- received_date
- received_by
- inspected_by
- quality_status

Quality Status:

- Accepted
- Accepted with Remarks
- Rejected

---

# Receiving Items

Each receiving line records:

- Item
- Quantity ordered
- Quantity received
- Unit
- Batch number
- Expiration date
- Actual cost

---

# Inventory Integration

Successful receiving automatically creates:

1. Inventory Batch

Example:

Calcium Nitrate

Batch:
CALN-2026-001

Quantity:
100 kg

---

2. Inventory Movement

Type:

Purchase Receiving

Quantity:

+100 kg

---

# Partial Deliveries

The system supports:

Example:

Purchase Order:

500 kg fertilizer

Delivery 1:

300 kg received

Status:
Partially Received


Delivery 2:

200 kg received

Status:
Completed

---

# Receiving Quality Control

The system shall record:

- Damaged items
- Incorrect quantities
- Expired materials
- Supplier quality issues

Rejected items shall not enter inventory.

---

# Approval Protection

High-value purchases may require:

- Supervisor approval
- Manager approval
- Owner approval

based on approval rules.

---

# Architecture Rule

No inventory quantity shall increase without a valid receiving record and corresponding inventory movement entry.
