# 20.25 Equipment Assets and Maintenance

## Purpose

The asset system manages company equipment, tools, machinery, and their maintenance history.

---

# Asset Lifecycle

Purchase
   ↓
Asset Registration
   ↓
Assignment
   ↓
Maintenance
   ↓
Repair
   ↓
Retirement

---

# equipment_assets Table

Fields:

Identification:

- id
- company_id
- branch_id
- asset_code
- asset_name
- category

Examples:

- Pumps
- Generators
- Vehicles
- Sensors
- Computers
- Farm Machinery

---

Asset Information:

- brand
- model
- serial_number
- purchase_date
- purchase_cost
- warranty_expiration

---

Operational Information:

- current_location
- assigned_to_user
- condition

Condition:

- Excellent
- Good
- Needs Maintenance
- Broken
- Retired

---

Financial Information:

- useful_life_years
- depreciation_method
- accumulated_depreciation
- current_book_value

---

Audit:

- created_by
- created_at
- updated_by
- updated_at

---

# Maintenance Records

Table:

equipment_maintenance_logs


Fields:

- id
- equipment_id
- maintenance_type

Examples:

- Preventive
- Corrective
- Emergency Repair

---

Maintenance Details:

- scheduled_date
- performed_date
- performed_by
- cost
- notes

---

# IoT Preparation

Equipment may support:

- Runtime monitoring
- Sensor health monitoring
- Automated maintenance alerts

---

# Architecture Rule

Every equipment asset shall maintain a complete lifecycle history from purchase to retirement.
