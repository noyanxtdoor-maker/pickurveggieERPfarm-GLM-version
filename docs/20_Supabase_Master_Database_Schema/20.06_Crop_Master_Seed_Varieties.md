# 20.06 Crop Master and Seed Varieties

## Purpose

This document defines the master database of crops, varieties, seed sources, and agricultural characteristics.

The system must distinguish between a crop category and a specific variety.

---

# Crop Classification Structure

Hierarchy:

Crop Category
       ↓
Crop
       ↓
Variety
       ↓
Seed Lot

Example:

Leafy Vegetables
       ↓
Lettuce
       ↓
Romaine Lettuce
       ↓
Lot #RM2026-001

# Seed Traceability

The system shall be able to answer:

Example:

Harvested Lettuce Batch #H001

Questions:

Which variety was planted?

Which seed lot was used?

Who supplied the seed?

When was it purchased?

What was its germination rate?

---

# Agricultural Intelligence Preparation

The crop database shall support future AI analysis:

Examples:

AI can recommend:

- Best crop for a location
- Planting schedules
- Yield predictions
- Disease risk warnings
- Fertilizer programs

---

# Multi-Branch Seed Management

Each branch maintains independent seed inventory.

Example:

Siargao Branch:

Romaine Lettuce Seed:
10,000 seeds

Cebu Branch:

Romaine Lettuce Seed:
3,000 seeds

The system shall prevent accidental mixing of inventory between branches.

---

# Architecture Rule

Every production cycle shall trace back to:

Crop
 ↓
Variety
 ↓
Seed Lot
 ↓
Supplier
 ↓
Purchase Record

Complete traceability must be maintained from seed to harvest.
