# 20.21 Chart of Accounts

## Purpose

The Chart of Accounts (COA) defines the financial structure of the company.

Every financial transaction must be classified using standardized accounts.

---

# Accounting Structure

The ERP follows double-entry accounting.

Accounting Equation:

Assets = Liabilities + Equity

---

# accounts Table

Fields:

- id
- company_id
- account_code
- account_name
- account_type

Account Types:

Assets:
- Cash
- Bank
- Accounts Receivable
- Inventory
- Equipment

Liabilities:
- Accounts Payable
- Loans
- Taxes Payable

Equity:
- Owner Capital
- Retained Earnings

Income:
- Vegetable Sales
- Service Revenue

Expenses:
- Labor Expense
- Fertilizer Expense
- Utility Expense
- Transportation Expense

---

Additional Fields:

- parent_account_id
- normal_balance
- active_status
- created_at
- updated_at

---

# Multi-Branch Accounting

A company uses one Chart of Accounts.

Financial transactions include:

- company_id
- branch_id

This allows:

- Branch profit reports
- Company consolidation
- Department analysis

---

# Architecture Rule

No financial transaction may exist without a valid account classification.
