# 20.13 Crop Blocks and Biological Assets

## Purpose

A crop block represents a specific group of plants grown together under the same production cycle.

A crop block is treated as a biological asset whose value, cost, growth, and yield can be monitored throughout its life cycle.

# Biological Asset Principle

A crop is a living asset.

The system shall monitor:

- Growth stage
- Survival rate
- Production cost
- Yield
- Quality grade

---

# Cost Accumulation

Example:

Lettuce Block LT-001:

Seeds:
₱1,000

Fertilizers:
₱3,000

Labor:
₱5,000

Total Cost:
₱9,000

The ERP shall accumulate all costs to the crop block.
