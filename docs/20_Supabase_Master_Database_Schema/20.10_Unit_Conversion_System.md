# 20.10 Unit Conversion System

## Purpose

The Unit Conversion System allows inventory, production, purchasing, and sales to use different measurement units while maintaining accurate inventory balances.

Examples:

- Fertilizer purchased in 25 kg bags
- Inventory tracked in kilograms
- Fertigation consumption recorded in grams

The system automatically converts between units.

---

---

# Inventory Base Unit Rule

Every inventory item shall have a single base unit.

Examples:

Calcium Nitrate:

Base Unit:
kg


Purchasing:
25 kg bags


Consumption:
grams


Inventory calculations shall always use the base unit.

---

# Conversion Integrity

Only compatible units may be converted.

Allowed:

kg ↔ g

L ↔ mL


Not Allowed:

kg ↔ liters

pieces ↔ kilograms

unless a specific product conversion is defined.

---

# Product-Specific Conversion Support

The system shall support custom conversions.

Examples:

1 Seed Tray = 200 Seedlings

1 Box of Lettuce = 10 kg

These conversions are linked to specific inventory items.

---

# Architecture Rule

All inventory calculations shall use standardized base units.

Display units may change, but stored quantities must remain consistent.
