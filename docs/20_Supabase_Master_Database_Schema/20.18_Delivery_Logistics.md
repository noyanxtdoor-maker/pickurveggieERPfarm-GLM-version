# 20.18 Delivery and Logistics

## Purpose

The logistics system controls how products move from branches to customers or between branches.

The system maintains complete delivery accountability.

---

# Delivery Types

The system supports:

- Customer Delivery
- Branch Transfer
- Supplier Return
- Internal Movement

---

# deliveries Table

Fields:

Identification:

- id
- company_id
- branch_id
- delivery_number

Reference Information:

- sales_order_id
- customer_id
- source_branch_id

---

Delivery Details:

Status:

- Scheduled
- Preparing

- Delivered

---

# Delivery Tracking

The system shall record:

- Departure time
- Arrival time
- Delivery confirmation
- Customer signature
- Delivery photos
- Delivery notes

---

# Branch Transfer Workflow

Example:

Siargao Branch:
Transfers 200 kg lettuce

        ↓

Cebu Branch:
Receives 200 kg lettuce

The system shall create:

Source branch:
- Inventory deduction

Destination branch:
- Inventory increase

Both movements remain linked.

---

# Cold Chain Support

The architecture supports future monitoring of:

- Transport temperature
- Humidity
- GPS location
- Delivery route

This is important for high-value perishable crops.

---

# Failed Deliveries

The system shall handle:

- Customer rejection
- Product damage
- Quantity discrepancy
- Return to inventory
- Disposal if unsafe

All outcomes must be recorded with full audit history.

---

# Architecture Rule

No product movement between locations or customers shall occur without a documented logistics record.
