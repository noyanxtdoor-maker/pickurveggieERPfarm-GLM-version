# 20.15 Harvest Batch Traceability

## Purpose

Every harvest receives a unique batch identity.

This enables complete farm-to-customer traceability.

---

# harvest_batches Table

Fields:

Identification:

- id
- company_id
- branch_id
- harvest_code

Source Information:

- crop_block_id
- crop_id
- variety_id

Harvest Details:

- harvest_date
- harvested_by

Quantity:

- gross_weight
- waste_weight
- net_weight

Quality:

- Grade A quantity
- Grade B quantity
- Reject quantity

---

Financial Information:

- production_cost
- cost_per_kg

---

Status:

- Fresh
- Packed
- Sold
- Expired
- Disposed

---

# Traceability Chain

A harvest must trace back to:

- Farm location
- Production zone
- Seed lot
- Production activities
- Fertilizers used
- Pesticides used
- Workers involved

---

# Food Safety Support

The system shall support:

- Product recalls
- Quality investigations
- Customer complaints

---

# Architecture Rule

No harvest shall exist without a linked crop block.
