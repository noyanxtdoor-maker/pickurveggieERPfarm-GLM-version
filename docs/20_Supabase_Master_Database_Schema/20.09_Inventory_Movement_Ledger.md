# 20.09 Inventory Movement Ledger

## Purpose

The inventory movement ledger is the permanent history of all stock changes.

It is the single source of truth for inventory balances.

Stock levels shall be calculated from movement history, not manually entered quantities.

---

# Movement Types

Inventory movement includes:

Inbound:

- Purchase Receiving
- Branch Transfer In
- Production Return
- Inventory Adjustment Increase

Outbound:

- Production Consumption
- Sales
- Branch Transfer Out
- Damage
- Expiration Disposal
- Inventory Adjustment Decrease

---

---

# Inventory Balance Calculation

Current inventory shall be calculated as:

Total Inbound Movements

minus

Total Outbound Movements

---

Transfer:

Movement 1:

Siargao

Type:
Transfer Out

Cebu

Type:

---

# Adjustment Protection

Inventory adjustments are high-risk operations.

Requirements:

- Reason is mandatory
- Approval workflow may be required
- Audit logs must be created

---

# Historical Integrity

Inventory movements shall never be deleted.

Mistakes shall be corrected by creating reversal or adjustment transactions.

---

# Architecture Rule

If inventory changed but no movement record exists, the system considers the inventory data invalid.
