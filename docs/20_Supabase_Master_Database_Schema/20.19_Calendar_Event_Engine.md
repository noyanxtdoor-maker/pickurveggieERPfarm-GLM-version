# 20.19 Calendar Event Engine

## Purpose

The Calendar Engine manages company and branch operational schedules.

It supports agricultural activities, maintenance schedules, meetings, deliveries, approvals, and company events.

---

# Calendar Ownership Levels

Events may belong to:

- Company-wide
- Branch-wide
- Department or production zone
- Specific project
- Specific equipment
- Specific crop block

---

- id (UUID)
- company_id
- branch_id

- event_type

- Planting

- Harvest
- Maintenance

- Meeting
- Inspection
- Training
- Deadline

---

# Automation Support

The system shall automatically create events from:

- Crop production schedules
- Equipment maintenance intervals
- Delivery schedules
- Approval deadlines

---

# Architecture Rule

Every operational activity requiring a schedule shall be managed through the calendar engine.
