# Pre-Production Checklist

Before public release:

## Technical

- Performance testing
- Security testing
- Backup procedures
- Disaster recovery testing

---

## Business

- Inventory audit passed
- Accounting reports validated
- User permissions tested

---

## Operations

- Field workers can operate offline
- Synchronization is reliable
- QR scanning works
- Audit logs are complete

---

Production deployment is not allowed until all checklist items pass.
