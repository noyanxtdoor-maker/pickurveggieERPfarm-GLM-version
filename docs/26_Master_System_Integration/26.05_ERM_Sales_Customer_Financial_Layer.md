# Sales, Customer and Financial Entity Relationship Layer

## Purpose

The sales and financial relationship layer manages how farm products move from harvest to customers and how those transactions affect the company's financial records.

The system must provide complete traceability from:

Crop Production

↓

Harvest

↓

Inventory

↓

Sales Order

↓

Delivery

↓

Customer Payment

↓

Accounting Records

---

## Purpose

Customers represent individuals or organizations that purchase products from the company.

- Resorts
- Hotels
- Retail buyers
- Market vendors
- Wholesalers
- Individual consumers

# Payment Records

## Important Principle

PickUrVeggie ERP does not hold actual money.

It records business transactions and payment confirmations.

---

# Supported Payment Methods

Version 1:

- Cash
- GCash
- Bank transfer
- Other manually verified methods

Future:

- Payment APIs
- Automatic bank confirmation
- QRPH integration
- Digital payment automation

---

# Manual Payment Verification Rule (Version 1)

For digital payments:

Customer says:

"Payment sent"

↓

Employee records:

Payment Awaiting Confirmation

↓

Authorized person checks company payment account

↓

Payment Verified

↓

ERP updates payment status

---

# Payment Status

Examples:

Pending

Customer has not paid.

---

Awaiting Verification

Customer claims payment was sent.

---

Verified

Authorized person confirms the payment.

---

Closed

Accounting reconciliation completed.

---

Disputed

Payment has problems requiring investigation.

---

# Sales Audit Principle

Every sale must answer:

Who bought the product?

↓

What product was sold?

↓

Where did it come from?

↓

When was it delivered?

↓

Who received payment?

↓

How did it affect accounting?

---

# Final Sales Principle

A sale is not complete when the product leaves the farm.

A sale is complete only when:

- The product is traceable
- The customer obligation is recorded
- The payment is verified
- The accounting impact is recorded
- The audit history is preserved
